# Design-partner outreach kit

Goal: land the first 10 to 25 paying (or soon-to-pay) sites from audiences we
already reach, so the growth agent has real traffic to optimize and we collect
testimonials and case studies. Do not buy ads yet. Distribution first, from
owned channels.

Rules of the road: no emojis, no em dashes, plain confident language. Lead with
the outcome (more customers), not the technology.

---

## The offer: Founding Sites program

Ten to twenty-five local businesses and creators get **Pro free for 3 months**
in exchange for:

1. A short testimonial and permission to use their site as a public example.
2. Letting the living-site growth agent run and reporting whether leads went up.
3. One 15-minute feedback call in week two.

Why this offer works: it removes price as a reason to say no, it gets real
traffic onto sites so the growth agent can prove itself, and it produces the
social proof that makes the next 100 sign-ups cheap.

Cap it at 25 so it stays scarce. After the cohort, everyone converts to the
$29.99/mo Pro plan or drops to Free.

## Who is a fit (target criteria)

- Runs a real local business or a creator brand that needs one clean site.
- Currently has no site, a dead Facebook page, or an abandoned Wix/Squarespace.
- Has some inbound already (so the growth agent has traffic to learn from).
- Values "it keeps improving itself" over "I want to hand-code it."

Best-fit verticals (the renderer already ships tuned themes for these):

- Trades and auto: plumbers, electricians, detailers, fabricators (industrial-slate)
- Makers and food: bakeries, ceramics, roasters, small-batch brands (terracotta-warmth)
- Growers and garden: florists, nurseries, mushroom and specialty ag (botanical-fresh)
- Wellness and hospitality: studios, spas, small hotels, cafes (coastal-calm)
- Salons and fine dining (nocturne-luxe)

## Where to find them (owned channels, in priority order)

1. **Southwest Mushrooms YouTube (195K)** community post + a pinned comment on
   the next upload. The audience skews growers, makers, and small food/ag
   businesses, which is dead center for the maker and grower themes.
2. **Skool community** and michaelcrowemycology.com members: these are people
   already building businesses around what they learn. Direct post plus DM to
   the most active members.
3. **Home-services contractors** already in the Crowe Agents Rainmaker pipeline:
   they need a site to point missed-call texts at. Natural cross-sell.
4. **Personal and Crowe Logic network**: one warm email beats fifty cold ones.

## Templates

### A. Email / DM (warm)

Subject: A free site that improves itself, for the first 25 of you

website in about a minute. The part I am proud of: once it is live, it watches
how visitors behave and quietly rewrites the weak sections to get you more
calls and orders. It does not just launch and sit there.

I am opening it to 25 founding businesses with Pro free for 3 months. All I ask
is a short testimonial and that you tell me honestly whether it moved the needle.

Want me to build yours? Reply with one sentence describing your business and I
will send back a live link today.

Michael

to have it first.

Describe your business in one sentence and it builds you a real website, live,
in about a minute. Then it keeps testing its own copy to win you more customers

the job.

Free Pro for 3 months for the first 25 who comment or message me. Trades,
makers, growers, cafes, studios, all welcome. Drop your one sentence below.

### C. Skool post

Founding Sites is open. If you run a business or a brand and your website is
missing, dead, or embarrassing, I will build you a live one from a single
sentence and give you Pro free for 3 months. In return I want your honest
feedback and a testimonial if it earns it. Comment your business in one line and
I will reply with a link.

### D. Cross-sell to Rainmaker contractors

You are already catching missed calls. The next lift is where those texts send
people. I will stand up a clean site for {company} that turns those clicks into
booked jobs, and it keeps improving its own copy as calls come in. Free for 3
months while you are in the founding group. Want me to build it today?

## Cadence and tracking

- Day 0: post to YouTube community + Skool, send 10 warm DMs.
- Day 2: reply to every comment with a live link built from their sentence.
- Day 7: follow up with anyone who got a link but did not publish.
- Day 14: 15-minute feedback calls; capture testimonials.
- Day 21: check `GET /api/admin/funnel` and each site's growth dashboard; write
  up the two best case studies.

Success metric for the cohort: 25 sites live, at least 10 with the growth agent
running on real traffic, at least 5 testimonials, and the first handful
converting to paid when the free window ends.
